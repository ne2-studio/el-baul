# PRODUCT.md

## Mission

Help families preserve, enrich and share their history so that no important
memory is lost over time.

## Vision

Within one hundred years, anyone should be able to open their family's *baúl*
and discover not only what their ancestors looked like, but who they were.

## Current stage

The product is currently **pre Product-Market Fit**.

Our primary goal is to learn as quickly as possible whether families are
willing to use El Baúl as part of their everyday life.

When making trade-offs:

- Prefer learning over completeness.
- Prefer shipping over polishing.
- Prefer simple solutions over scalable ones.
- Avoid adding complexity before it is justified by real usage.

## Target users

Primary users:

- Parents.
- Grandparents.
- Family members acting as the historian or custodian of the family.

Secondary users:

- Adult children.
- Other invited relatives.

## Core problem

Photos preserve what happened.

They rarely preserve:

- who was there,
- what people felt,
- why the moment mattered,
- the stories surrounding it.

El Baúl exists to preserve those stories before they disappear.

## Product principles

### Stories are the product

Photos, *personas* and *capítulos* exist to support stories.

When in doubt, optimize for creating and discovering meaningful stories.

### Capture first, organize later

Never require users to organize information before saving it.

Reducing friction is more important than maintaining perfect organization.

### Help users remember

Users should never feel forced to remember everything.

The product should actively help them recover forgotten memories.

### Family before social

El Baúl is a private family space.

Features that encourage family conversations are preferred over features
designed for public sharing or engagement.

### Preserve authenticity

Never fabricate facts.

AI may suggest, infer or ask questions, but it must clearly distinguish
between known facts and assumptions.

## Success metrics

North Star Metric:

- Active families.

Important product KPIs:

- Families that return weekly.
- Memories created.
- Stories completed.
- Photos uploaded.
- Invitations accepted.
- Active contributors per family.

## Product priorities

Current priorities are:

1. Capture family memories.
2. Enrich those memories with context.
3. Encourage collaboration inside the family.
4. Help users rediscover forgotten memories.

Lower priority:

- Advanced organization.
- Customization.
- Power-user workflows.
- Large-scale administration.

## Non-goals

El Baúl is not:

- A cloud photo storage service.
- A genealogy application.
- A social network.
- A generic note-taking application.

## AI principles

AI exists to help families remember.

It should:

- ask meaningful questions;
- connect existing memories;
- surface forgotten context;
- inspire new stories.

It should never invent family history or present speculation as fact.

## Glossary

### Baúl

A private space shared by a family.

### Capítulo

A meaningful period, event or theme in the family's history.

### Story

A narrative composed from one or more *recuerdos*.

### Recuerdo

A recollection attached to a photo, *capítulo* or *persona*.

### Persona

Someone who belongs to the family's history.

A *persona* does not need to be a registered user.

### Custodio

The family member responsible for creating and managing a *baúl*.
